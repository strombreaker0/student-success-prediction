Batch Screening Methodology

Batch screening enables educators and academic committees to rapidly scan large cohorts and prioritize remedial action plans prior to examination periods.

  • Automated stratification into Low Risk, Moderate Risk, and High Risk tiers.
  • Identifies students falling below the 75% attendance benchmark or 12 hours/week study threshold.
  • Outputs a sorted priority list for early-warning faculty intervention.
