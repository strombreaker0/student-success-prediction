System & Model Governance Limitations
1. Cohort Sample Scope

Calibrated on an undergraduate computer science cohort. Predictions require calibration when transferred to distinct academic departments or non-credit courses.

2. Unmodeled Qualitative Dynamics

Features capture quantitative metrics (attendance percentage, study hours, sleep duration). External events like health emergencies or socioeconomic stress must be evaluated by advisors.

3. Advisory Decision-Support Only

The models function as assistive early-warning pipelines. Faculty intervention and individualized student mentoring must always guide the final pedagogical decisions.
