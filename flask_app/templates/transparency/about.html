Mathematical Formulation
Logistic Regression Hypothesis
P(Y = 1 | x) = σ(wTx + b) = 1 / (1 + e-(wTx + b))

Where w represents the learned weights vector for the 8 behavioral features, and b is the bias intercept.

Standard Scaling (Z-Score Normalization)
Z = (X - μ) / σ

Features are standardized to zero mean (μ) and unit variance (σ) to ensure metric distance equilibrium across heterogeneous indicators.
